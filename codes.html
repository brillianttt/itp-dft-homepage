Codes & Computational Tools

We develop numerical and first-principles tools for quantum materials, focusing on Berry curvature, transport properties, and electronic structure calculations.

Quantum DFT Package

A first-principles electronic structure package for correlated quantum materials.

Problem: Accurate band structure and orbital-resolved analysis in complex materials.
Method: Density functional theory with symmetry-adapted basis expansion.

📄 Related work: Phys. Rev. B (2026)

Quantum Transport Solver

Numerical solver for Berry-curvature-driven transport phenomena.

Problem: Anomalous Hall and thermoelectric responses beyond semiclassical theory.
Method: Kubo formula + quantum geometric tensor formalism.

📄 Related work: PRB / PRL (in preparation)

Berry Curvature Toolkit

A computational toolkit for quantum geometry and topological invariants.

Problem: Efficient evaluation of Berry curvature and quantum metric.
Method: Wannier interpolation + Brillouin zone sampling optimization.

📄 Related work: Topology & Geometry in Condensed Matter
