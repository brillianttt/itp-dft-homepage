Welcome to Zhang's Group

Institute of Theoretical Physics | ITP-DFT Research

We are a research group focused on theoretical condensed matter physics and first-principles calculations. Our work centers on quantum geometric phenomena in diverse quasiparticle systems and across parameter spaces, alongside investigations of thermoelectric properties and related effects in solid-state materials.
