Research

Overview

We explore theoretical condensed matter physics and first-principles simulations. Our research focuses on quantum geometric effects in quasiparticle systems, band topology, and thermoelectric transport in quantum materials.

Research Directions

First-Principles Theory

Density functional theory and ab initio modeling of quantum materials.

Topological Quantum Matter

Topological phases, Dirac/Weyl fermions, and band geometry.

Quantum Geometry

Berry curvature, quantum metric, and geometric responses.

Thermoelectric Transport

Electronic transport and thermal response in correlated materials.

Recent Highlight

Quantum geometric origin of anomalous transport in correlated systems.

Published in Physical Review B (2026). This work reveals how quantum geometry governs transport responses beyond conventional semiclassical theory.

Future Directions

We are currently extending our framework to non-equilibrium quantum dynamics, strongly correlated systems, and light-induced topological phases.
