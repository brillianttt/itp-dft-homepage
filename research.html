Research

Overview

Our group focuses on theoretical condensed matter physics and quantum materials, with emphasis on density functional theory (DFT), topological phases, and nonequilibrium quantum phenomena.

Research Topics

Density Functional Theory

Electronic structure calculations and materials modeling.

Topological Materials

Topological phases, Dirac/Weyl systems and band topology.

Nonequilibrium Physics
